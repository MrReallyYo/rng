How the calculations work

Effective power = P × [1 + (C / 100) × (D / 100 - 1)]

P is Power, C is critical chance in percent, and D is total critical damage in percent. Normal hits contribute P; critical hits contribute P × D / 100. The formula takes their probability-weighted average.

15 Ferocity adds 1 percentage point of critical damage. One point of Power adds 1 + c(D / 100 - 1) effective power; one point of Ferocity adds Pc / 1500. The recommendation compares these gains, where c = C / 100. Equal Power/Ferocity additions break even when P = 15D + 1500(1 - c) / c.

At 100% crit, the equal-budget rule is P = 15D. At 75% crit, it is P = 15D + 500. Below the target, favor Power; above it, favor Ferocity.

In the complete-build comparison, the winner's percentage advantage is relative to the other build's effective power. Values are rounded only for display.

This is an average strike-damage metric, not DPS. It excludes condition damage, on-crit effects, special trait interactions, rotation changes, and differences in weapon strength, skill coefficients or other damage modifiers. Crit chance must be 0–100%. All calculations stay in your browser.
